Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a specific building on campus?

Go to the Directory page and search for the building name. You can also open the interactive map to view directions.

Can I reserve a room online?

Yes. Browse available rooms, choose a schedule, and submit your reservation request directly from GoldenPath.

How do I report an issue or submit feedback?

Use the Contact Support section or the feedback form available inside the Help Center.

Who do I contact for accessibility concerns?

Please contact the Student Affairs Office or email our support team for assistance.
